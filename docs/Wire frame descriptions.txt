WireFrame1 description: At top displays race name and current race time, it additionally gives runback, or playback speed options and the opportunity to go to the start of a different lap whilst also displaying current lap using a drop down menu. There is a section for text commentary fed from the AI. The track map is displayed, and will show the cars position on that map. Several different information graphs are sectioned together, and they will be dynamically changing, or alternating with other graphs to show most relevant information. The timeline at the bottom displays how far through the race you are, also clearly showing each beginning of new lap, it will also let you skip to any point in the race.
